How do I add Cyder to my Discord server?
+

Simply click the "Add to Discord" button anywhere on the website or navigate to our authentication gateway. Make sure you have "Manage Server" permissions in the target guild to authorize the bot successfully.

Is Cyder completely free to use?
+

Yes! Our Free tier offers Many core features! our premiums are also available for advanced functions enroll now!

What is the difference between Guild Pro and User Premium?
+

Guild Pro upgrades your entire server so every member gets access to premium filters and features. User Premium follows your personal Discord ID across any server where Cyder is active.

What should I do if the bot stops Working ?
+

First, try re-inviting the bot or checking if it has proper permissions granted in the server!
